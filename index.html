Triage gate

Six questions, asked in this order, during the triage call. The first miss decides the call. Answer honestly and the bottom bar tells you exactly what to do and what to tag in the OS.

0 of 6 answered

1. Have they led a tech team?

Ask: Tell me about the last team you led. How many engineers or product people, for how long, and what did you ship?

Yes means: 3+ years leading software, product or engineering people or architecture — a hands-on IC, a networks/hardware-only role, or a marketer who also builds websites is a no.

2. Are they open to running their own practice?

Ask: What are you hoping to get out of this — are you looking to build your own fractional CTO practice, or something else?

Yes means: they want their own practice, or they are still deciding between a W-2 and a practice. Undecided is a strategy call, not a disqualifier — connecting them to the vision is the closer’s job. No means: they want an agency model, a placement only, a lead list only, or an MSP-style service.

3. Do they understand what fractional CTO is and how we work?

Give the 30-second explainer first (fCTO vs MSP, mentorship not job placement, what the program delivers). Then ask: Does that match what you were picturing?

Yes means: they heard the explainer and still want in. If they need more time to understand the model, that is a not-right-now, not a strategy call.

4. Can they invest?

Ask: Our programs run between $3K and $15K depending on the track. Is that something you're able to invest in right now?

Yes means: they say yes to that range now. A yes to the money but not for a quarter is later. Debt, no income, or a flat no is no.

5. Are they ready to start in the next 30 days?

Ask: On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready are you to move on this in the next 30 days?

Yes means: 7 or above. Anything under 7 is exploring, and exploring is not a strategy call.

6. Can they decide alone?

Ask: If the strategy call goes well, who else needs to be part of the decision?

Yes means: nobody, or the other person will be on the strategy call. A partner or spouse who has to sign off and won't be there is a no.

Thresholds (3 years, $3K–$15K, 7 of 10, 30 days) are taken from the Sales Coach weekly posts and October’s DNQ reasons. They are proposals until Gerard or Donna confirms them; once confirmed, this is the definition of qualified.
